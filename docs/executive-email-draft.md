**Subject:** Container Loading Application — UAT Readiness

Dear Khun TK,

Following your request, we have prepared a Container Loading application to improve how our teams document truck loading activities. The application guides team members through recording truck and shipment details, capturing supporting photos, and closing each trip once the required information is complete.

Each trip is associated with one store and one shipment, and can include up to 120 photos. The operations team can review trip records and images through an administrator dashboard, with options to view, download, and print a photo report. The application also supports archiving older trip photos while retaining the trip records for reference.

The next step is User Acceptance Testing (UAT) at HDC. We plan to verify the end-to-end workflow on the Zebra handheld device and administrator workstation, including barcode input, camera and plate recognition, photo uploads, trip completion, and access to reports. We will use the UAT results to resolve any issues before production deployment.

Please let us know which HDC team members should participate in UAT and which operating scenarios should be included. We will share the test schedule and access details once the UAT environment is ready.
